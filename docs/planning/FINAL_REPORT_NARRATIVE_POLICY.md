# Final Report Narrative Policy

Career Learning Hub should be presented in university-facing material as one integrated authenticated system with Dashboard, Resume Studio, Interview Coach, Learning Workspace and controlled Gemini-assisted processing.

The final report should focus on the project problem, requirements, design, implementation, evaluation, limitations and final evidence. Internal development-source history is not part of the final academic narrative.

Historical PID and Interim evidence remain valid for their submission checkpoints. Final evidence should be drawn from the final release records rather than retroactively altering earlier checkpoint claims.
