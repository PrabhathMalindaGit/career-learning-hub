# Career Learning Hub brand usage

The approved identity is the **Open Book + Rising Pathway** symbol with the
wordmark **Career Learning Hub**. Use the production assets in
`frontend/public/brand/`; do not reconstruct the logo from screenshots.

## Approved colours

| Role | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Primary forest green | `#287a4a` |
| Dark forest green | `#1e6039` |
| Ink | `#18211b` |
| Light surface | `#f4f7f5` |
| White | `#ffffff` |

Use solid fills only. Gradients, textures, shadows, gloss, and 3D treatments
are not part of the production identity.

## Clear space and size

Keep clear space around a complete lockup equal to one symbol height on every
side. Do not allow text, borders, or other graphics into that area.

The practical digital minimum is 90 px wide for the horizontal logo and 45 px
wide for the standalone symbol. The dedicated favicon is the approved
small-size exception and is designed for 16, 24, and 32 px browser contexts.

## Light and dark surfaces

Use `career-learning-hub.svg` or `career-learning-hub-mark.svg` on white and
`#f4f7f5` surfaces. Use `career-learning-hub-reversed.svg` on dark green,
ink, or comparably dark solid surfaces. Use
`career-learning-hub-monochrome.svg` only when production is restricted to a
single colour.

## Prohibited modifications

Do not stretch, skew, rotate, crop, recolour, outline, or add effects. Do not
alter the open-book silhouette or rising pathway. Do not change the wordmark,
rearrange its elements, or add initials, letters, graduation caps, AI
symbols, arrows, stars, or other imagery.

## Accessibility

When the symbol sits beside visible “Career Learning Hub” text, use an empty
image alternative (`alt=""`) so the visible text supplies the accessible
name once. Give an interactive standalone logo an accessible name describing
its destination or action. Preserve the application’s visible focus
indicator on logo links. Do not place essential information only inside a
logo image.
